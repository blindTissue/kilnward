# KILNWARD — Storyline Outline & Lore Bible

This document defines the complete narrative arc across **Acts I–IV**, character voices, worldbuilding rules, and exact hooks for future agents continuing development beyond the playable Act I demo.

---

## 1. High-Concept Premise

The vertical cliff-abbey of **Orrery** was founded by the **Order of the Bell-Makers** to conquer grief: when a citizen died, their final memories were fired into glazed white porcelain vessels and sealed with molten Kintsugi gold, then attuned to the **Great Choir of Bells** so the living could hear their counsel.

Seven years before the game begins, the **First Kiln** deep in the sunken foundations cracked. Its volatile fuel—**Cinder-Ichor**—boiled upward through the aqueducts and mortuaries, over-firing thousands of stored vessels into mindless, burning **Hollow Vessels** and forcing the upper tiers of Orrery to seal the lower cathedral shut.

You play as **Vesper**, an awakened **Kintsugi Mender** whose own porcelain body was patched together with stubborn gold seams. Armed with a crescent kiln-blade and an ember-lantern, Vesper must climb from the flooded foundations to the sky-belfry, deciding whether Orrery's immortal kiln should be **re-sealed**, **extinguished**, or **recast**.

---

## 2. Core Themes & Narrative Rules

- **Beauty in Repair, Horror in Refusal to End:** Kintsugi honors fracture and impermanence; the abbey's tragedy came from firing souls *too hot* so they could never break or rest.
- **Sparse, Evocative Prose:** Follow the restraint of *Swordigo*, *Diablo I*, and *Dark Souls*. Dialogues should be 2–3 tight paragraphs max; lore steles should reveal both history *and* an emotional truth about the upcoming boss or mechanic.
- **No Comic Relief Quips:** Tone is elegiac, warm in sanctuary moments, reverent toward the fallen saints, and crisp during urgent warnings.

## 3. Four-Act Storyline Outline

### ACT I — *The Sunken Kiln* **(IMPLEMENTED IN DEMO)**
- **Setting:** The lowest flooded/scorched cathedral cross beneath Orrery (The Sunken Cloister, Bell-Crossroads, Verdigris Nave, Glaze Ossuary, Crown Crucible).
- **Inciting Event:** Vesper awakens in the Cloister. **Brother Oren**, a cracked porcelain bell-keeper, explains that the Sanctum Gate is locked until the **Twin Resonance Bells** sound together.
- **Gameplay Arc:**
  1. Explore the **Bell-Crossroads** and fight initial Cracked Husks & Censer Acolytes.
  2. Traverse the **Verdigris Nave (West)**, solve the **Resonance Crystal chasm puzzle** with Arcane Bolt, defeat a Reliquary Sentinel, unlock **Solar Kiln Nova (`Q`)** & **Verdigris Signet**, and ring the **West Resonance Bell**.
  3. Enter the **Glaze Ossuary (East)**, survive the **2-wave Crucible Ambush** while dodging periodic furnace ember vents, unlock **Kintsugi Aegis (`R`)** & **Bell-Keeper's Clapper**, and ring the **East Resonance Bell**.
  4. Both golden resonance sky-beams unseal the **Sanctum Gate**. Rest/temper at the Kiln Shrine, then enter the **Crown Crucible**.
- **Act I Boss:** **Saint Cassian, The Fractured Kiln** — the abbey's former Warden who braced the cracked furnace with his own body so the city above could flee, fusing himself to the heat.
- **Climax & Act II Hook:** Shattering Cassian's over-fired shell frees his quiet **Mended Echo**, who gifts Vesper the *Shard of Cassian's Glaze* and rings the **Great First Bell**, cooling the stairwell upward to **The Salt Viaduct**.

---

### ACT II — *The Salt Viaduct* **(NEXT MILESTONE FOR FUTURE AGENTS)**
- **Setting:** Wind-swept white salt-brick aqueducts and suspended porcelain bridges spanning the cliff face above the sunken abbey. Palette shifts to **bleak moonlit salt-white (`#e8edf2`), storm-indigo sky, and corroded cobalt banners**.
- **Narrative Focus:** Vesper discovers that the upper city did *not* merely evacuate—the **High Glaziers** deliberately flooded the lower abbey to hide a failed experiment: they tried to fire a *living* Arch-Prelate into immortal porcelain, which is what cracked the First Kiln.
- **New NPC:** **Sister Maren**, a blind salt-cartographer camping in a bell-tower tollbooth who trades rare **Glaze Charms** (build-defining passive modifiers) and unlocks fast-travel between lit Kiln Shrines.
- **New Mechanics & Spells:**
  - **Wind-Gust Bridges & Counterweight Bell Puzzles:** Ringing suspended counterweight bells rotates bridge segments.
  - **Unlockable Spell 4 — Verdigris Tether (Hook/Blink):** Grapple across viaduct gaps or pull airborne casters into melee range (expanding the Swordigo-style traversal toolkit).
- **Act II Boss:** **The Twin Arch-Censers (Vael & Soren)** — a synchronized duo fight on a narrow aqueduct span (one melee chain-censer, one sniper lantern; defeating one empowers the survivor with Kintsugi lightning).

---

### ACT III — *The Cobalt Cisterns & The Mosaic Library*
- **Setting:** Subterranean blue-and-gold majolica waterworks and sunken scriptoria where living memory-tablets are stored in tall rotating bronze cylinders. Palette: **deep lapis blue (`#142a5c`), specular turquoise water reflections, and luminous ivory shelves**.
- **Narrative Focus:** Vesper uncovers the true origin of their own body: Vesper was the **apprentice of Saint Cassian**, shattered during the evacuation and secretly mended by Brother Oren using fragments of *three* fallen Bell-Makers—which is why Vesper can channel multiple resonance arts.
- **New Mechanics:**
  - **Reflective Mirror-Tile Beams:** Redirect golden kiln light beams using rotatable bronze mirrors to unlock the Archivist's Vault.
  - **Weapon Aspect Switching:** Switch between *Crescent Claymore* (balanced) and *Bell-Hammer* (slow poise-breaker) at any Kiln Shrine.
- **Act III Boss:** **Archivist Pelagius, The Kiln-Orrery** — a towering multi-armed porcelain astrolabe suspended over a flooded library floor that rotates safe floor tiles between phases.

---

### ACT IV — *The Belfry of Unfired Ash* (Finale)
- **Setting:** The sunlit dawn peak of Orrery's cathedral spire, where wind scatters white ash against a pale amber sky—breaking the subterranean darkness for the first time in the entire game!
- **Narrative Focus:** At the summit sits **Arch-Prelate Aurelius, The Uncooled**, still encased in a blinding kiln-throne fed by every bell below. He believes breaking the kiln means erasing centuries of human memory.
- **Final Choice (Multiple Endings):**
  1. **Ending A — *The Golden Seam* (Mend & Cool):** Temper the First Kiln down to gentle warmth—preserving memories while allowing souls to pass on naturally.
  2. **Ending B — *The Silent Ash* (Shatter the Kiln):** Extinguish Cinder-Ichor forever, freeing all Hollow Vessels into true rest (including Vesper).
  3. **Ending C — *The Eternal Choir* (Secret, requires all Lore Steles):** Re-attune the Great Bells using Cassian's Shard so Orrery becomes a sanctuary of living song rather than trapped porcelain.

## 4. Key Character Dossiers

| Character | Role | Visual Signature | Speech Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vesper** | Silent Protagonist (Kintsugi Mender) | Midnight indigo cloak, gold-seamed porcelain mask, crescent blade, ember lantern | Silent in dialogue; player choices are concise, resolute vows |
| **Brother Oren** | Act I Hub Guide | Teal robe, cracked porcelain face, conical gold bell-hat, bell staff | Warm, anxious yet steadfast; uses kiln/ceramic metaphors ("hold your glaze", "ring true") |
| **Saint Cassian** | Act I Boss / Tragic Martyr | Monumental furnace-cored warden with horned gold mitre and crucible glaive | Silent during combat; Echo speaks softly, weary and grateful for cool air |
| **Sister Maren** *(Act II)* | Salt Cartographer & Charm Vendor | Weathered white salt-cloak, brass astrolabe cane, braided copper veil | Crisp, skeptical of the High Glaziers, fascinated by acoustics |
| **Arch-Prelate Aurelius** *(Act IV)* | Final Antagonist | Blinding white-gold porcelain throne-knight radiating molten filigree | Liturgical, grandiose, genuinely terrified of forgetting |
